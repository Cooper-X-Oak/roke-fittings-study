# Unofficial educational mirror

This repository and its GitHub Pages site are an unofficial educational mirror
of the public frontend at `https://www.roke-fittings.ru/`.

ROKE names, logos, product images, documents, videos, and other source-site
materials belong to their respective rights holders. This mirror must not be
presented as the official website, used to collect personal information, or
used for commercial purposes.

The static deployment does not implement the source site's Bitrix/PHP forms,
AJAX endpoints, database, analytics, or other server-side behavior.
