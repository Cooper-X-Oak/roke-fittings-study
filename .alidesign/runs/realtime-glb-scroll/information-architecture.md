# Information architecture

## Accepted structure

The experience is one continuous study rather than a conventional marketing page:

1. **Context** — identifies the work as a realtime 3D experiment and explains the input.
2. **Assembly** — presents the intact product and loading state.
3. **Rotation** — turns the product to prove live geometry rather than frame playback.
4. **Separation** — moves meshes outward using authored part structure and spatial vectors.
5. **Understanding** — holds the exploded pose long enough to inspect compression and rendering facts.
6. **Credits** — names the asset, authors, license, and unofficial educational scope.

## Desktop layout

- The canvas is viewport-fixed.
- Chapters occupy ordinary document flow over approximately five viewports.
- Copy uses alternating clear zones but never crosses the central model focal area.
- A top progress rule and right technical rail remain persistent.
- The final credits block is readable over a quiet surface without relying on canvas content.

## Narrow and failure layout

- Sticky positioning and oversized scroll length are removed.
- The stage becomes a bounded 60vh panel.
- Chapters and facts follow in normal flow.
- Unsupported/error states replace only the canvas, not the entire page.

## Entry, return, and recovery

- Direct entry starts at the assembled state.
- Native back/forward and reload behavior are preserved.
- Reversing scroll is the recovery path from any model pose.
- Motion can be paused without losing chapter content.
