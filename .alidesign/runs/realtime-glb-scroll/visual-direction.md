# Visual direction

## Governing sources

1. Current user request and Issue #3 acceptance criteria.
2. Repository educational/unofficial constraints.
3. Inspected local mirror screenshot: light-gray studio, large charcoal wordform, red accent, isolated metal product.
4. `$review-design-quality` for general coherence and accessibility.

## Core concept

**Digital teardown table.** The page behaves like a clean industrial inspection bay: one object, one neutral light environment, one red calibration mark, and just enough technical annotation to explain what the browser is doing.

## Signature element

The live concept car changes from a complete product silhouette to a legible exploded construction through direct scroll. No secondary illustration competes with it.

## Composition

- Oversized charcoal title sits partially behind the product in the opening state, echoing the mirror's product-over-wordmark composition without copying its logo.
- The car occupies roughly 55-65% of the desktop width.
- Red appears only in the progress rail, active chapter marker, and small status details.
- Hairlines and monospaced metadata suggest calibration equipment; no card grid or ornamental glass.

## Material and light

- Neutral RoomEnvironment-generated reflections with an elevated key and soft fill.
- ACES filmic tone mapping and physically correct color management.
- Subtle ground plane/contact shadow anchors the car.
- Preserve the model's authored clearcoat, transmission, iridescence, and emissive materials.

## Conflict resolution

The source mirror's exact logo, contact information, product content, and proprietary model are outside authority. Only its general industrial composition is applicable; Khronos asset identity and attribution remain explicit.
