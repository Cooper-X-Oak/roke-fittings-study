## Problem and intended outcome

The current mirror demonstrates scroll-driven disassembly with 650 pre-rendered AVIF frames. The experiment must test the alternative proposed by the user: one real-time, GPU-rendered product model whose rotation and disassembly are driven by scroll, with materially lower transfer and GPU-resident KTX2 textures.

## Applicable authority

- User direction: desktop WebGL, compressed GLB, KTX2, scroll rotation and exploded view.
- Project policy: `AGENTS.md` and `docs/engineering/github-development.md`.
- Visual baseline: inspected `artifacts/home-local.png` from the current educational mirror.
- Asset authority: Khronos glTF Sample Assets `CarConcept` README and license.
- General craft fallback: `$review-design-quality`.
