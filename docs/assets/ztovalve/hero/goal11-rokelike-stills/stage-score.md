# Goal 11 ROKE-like Hero Still Direction

## Authority

- User-approved direction: ROKE-like motion should be restrained. The subject should remain stable, movement should follow fixed axes, parts should move as explanatory groups, and the final assembly can use a controlled small rotation.
- Output boundary: six still frames only. Do not render a 240-frame sequence in Goal 11.
- Product facts allowed: ZTOVALVE / 正拓阀门, flanged and threaded valve product entry, ball valves, gate valves, globe valves, check valves, filters, customer-supplied fixed ball valve STEP.
- No-claim boundary: do not state verified material grade, pressure class, certification, fire-safe behavior, anti-static behavior, leakage class, service life, or maintenance procedure without client approval.
- Reference boundary: ROKE is a grammar reference for stable B2B industrial presentation, not a source to copy assets, claims, text, or brand identity.

## Direction

- Thesis: A procurement visitor should read ZTOVALVE as a stable valve manufacturer before noticing the fixed ball valve mechanism.
- Viewer arc: complete product presence -> restrained axial alignment -> controlled structural cue -> assembled product turn -> stable purchase-oriented hero hold.
- Dominant staged-image rule: every still must work as a stable homepage screenshot; no in-between frame should look like a scattered CAD explosion.
- Camera grammar: one steady three-quarter observation angle, no roll, no free orbit, no large push-in.
- Product grammar: left/right axial separation is small; small hardware remains product texture; central axis cue is brief and contained.
- Typography grammar: title and CTA serve product discovery, not technical explanation.
- Lighting: light industrial grey stage, dark steel body, controlled silver edge highlights, visible contact shadow.
- Negative list: no copper/plastic look, no loose fastener flight, no full transparent shell stack, no cropped product hero, no unsupported performance claims.

Proceed to a 24-frame low-resolution animatic only if the six stills pass these gates:
