Modifications in this experiment:
- Repacked the glTF-KTX-BasisU-Draco variant into a single GLB container.
- Re-encoded Draco geometry with 10-bit position, normal, texture-coordinate,
  and generic attribute quantization.
- Preserved the KTX2/BasisU texture payloads and authored materials.
- Applied runtime transforms to named part groups for the exploded-view study.
